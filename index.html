Hi, I'm Diogo! I'm a postdoctoral researcher at the Copenhagen Center for Social Data Science (SODAS).

My research focuses on how social behaviour, interaction structure, and mobility shape social and biological systems. I am currently developing new methods for comparing empirical social network data against theoretical models, with applications to both human and non-human sociality.
